===== PAGE 3 =====
We can achieve this through Sealed classes/interfaces in java. 
Tells that, it can be subclassed but only by its own "permits"
list 
"final" tells that it can not be  
subclassed further. 
"non-sealed" removes all restriction and can
be subclassed freely. 
Few things to consider are: 
 
• "permits" type should be direct subclass of sealed interface or class.
